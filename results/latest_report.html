Research protocol

Stanford HIVDB filtered in-vitro phenotype datasets; drug-specific models; four disjoint partitions (train, validation, calibration, test), grouped by shared SeqID and exact within-gene mutation signatures. The unseen test partition is never used to select fusion weights, intervals or novelty thresholds. Prediction target: log2 of reported fold-change in susceptibility versus wild-type reference.

Comparators: training-set median; mutation ridge; position-only ExtraTrees ablation; full-mutation ExtraTrees; validation-weighted ensemble. Two conformal approaches are compared: constant-width and genotype-novelty-scaled. No exchangeability guarantee is asserted under distribution shift.

Connected groups guard against exact-genotype leakage but cannot guarantee patient-disjoint splits because patient identifiers are not present. Reported performance is isolate-level in vitro only.

Artifacts and checks

All numerical results are in tables/; split assignments and sample-level held-out predictions are preserved. Each fitted drug-level model has a checkpoint in checkpoints/. Use the checkpoint directory with care: it is research software, not a clinical device.

Skipped or failed experiments: 5. Investigate any skips before making manuscript claims. Models should be compared within the same drug/seed; averaging across drugs gives equal weight to each experiment and differs from pooled sample-level RMSE.
